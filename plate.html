What I learned

Divergent: plates move apart. Magma can form new crust.
Convergent: plates move together. Mountains, volcanoes, or trenches can form.
Transform: plates slide past each other. This can cause earthquakes.
Pangaea: a supercontinent that contained most of Earth’s land.
Continental drift: the idea that continents move over time.
Alfred Wegener: the scientist who proposed continental drift.
Evidence: matching fossils, similar animals, matching rocks and mountains, and coastlines that fit together.
